# 1. CORE STRATEGIC POSITIONING

## What Akeso Is

Akeso is a vision technology and deeptech research company focused on advanced optics, precision visual systems, and future-facing human vision technologies.

The company combines:

- Ophthalmology-led understanding of human vision
- Precision optical engineering
- Advanced manufacturing
- Visual intelligence systems
- Computational optics
- XR-enabling optical infrastructure

Akeso should NOT be positioned as:

- An eyewear company
- A healthcare provider
- A generic AI startup
- An XR gadget company
- A consumer electronics brand
- A medical device company

Instead, Akeso should feel like:

- A precision optics and visual systems company
- A research-driven technology platform
- A future-facing visual technology infrastructure company
- A company engineering foundational technology for the future of human vision

---

# 2. CORE BRAND PRINCIPLES

## Strategic Narrative

Technology should adapt to human vision — not force human vision to adapt to technology.

Akeso’s advantage comes from building visual technologies from an ophthalmic and optical understanding of the eye itself.

This becomes the philosophical foundation of the website.
