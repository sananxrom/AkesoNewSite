Ai+ Visual Intelligence

Xr+ Future Vision Platforms

AKESO explores optical technologies supporting emerging visual platforms — immersive systems, wearable visual computing, and advanced display architectures. Research focuses on improving visual comfort, focal behavior, and long-duration visual interaction. The Xr+ platform includes a proprietary far-distance virtual image display system designed to support myopia prevention.

Focus XR optical systems & far-focus visual architectures

Application Myopia prevention display systems & future visual computing
04 — System Philosophy

Technology should
adapt to human vision.

AKESO's research philosophy is built around the belief that visual technologies should align more naturally with human visual behavior — not force adaptation around hardware constraints.

This perspective influences the company's approach to optics, display systems, visual interfaces, and future immersive technologies. Each platform — MetaRx™, Ai+, and Xr+ — is grounded in ophthalmic science and unified by this guiding principle.

01 Ophthalmic science as the starting point — not hardware constraints
02 Precision engineering informed by human visual behavior
03 Research-led development across optics, AI, and display systems
04 Long-term foundational thinking — not product catalog iteration
